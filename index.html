Welcome to Built-Rite Construction

Built-Rite Construction & Sons LLC is a family owned and operated general contracting company serving the tri-county area for over twenty years. Dave Mason started the company in 1987 out of the back of his pickup truck, and today he works alongside his sons Dave Jr. and Mike on every job we take on.

From small repairs to complete custom homes, we handle it all — and we show up when we say we will. We are fully licensed, bonded and insured, and all of our work is guaranteed in writing. Take a look around, and give us a call when you're ready. We look forward to earning your buisness.
