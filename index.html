◆ About Our Company ◆

Welcome to the website of BUILT-RITE CONSTRUCTION & SONS LLC!!! We are a family owned and operated full service general contracting company that has been proudly serving the tri-county area and surrounding areas for over 20 years. My name is Dave and I started this buisness out of the back of my truck in 1987 with nothing but a hammer and a dream. Today me and my sons Dave Jr. and Little Mike do EVERYTHING from small repairs all the way up to complete custom homes. There is NO JOB TO SMALL and NO JOB TO BIG!!! We take pride in our quality workmenship and our attention to detail and our competitive pricing which is why 9 out of 10 of our customers say they would recomend us to a friend or family member. We are fully LICENSED, BONDED and INSURED for your protection and peace of mind. We show up when we say we are going to show up which is more than you can say for the other guys. All of our work is guaranteed. Please take a look around our website and if you have any questions at all please dont hesitate to give us a call or send us a e-mail and one of our friendly staff will get back to you usually within 24-48 hours or the next buisness day. We look forward to earning your buisness!!!
