The one thing

A wall almost never fails at the brick.

It fails at the joint. Water finds the mortar first, gets behind the face, and waits for a Wisconsin winter to freeze and expand. Do that a few hundred times and the brick face lets go. Every job we take, new or two hundred years old, is really about closing the path water uses to get in.

Try it

Press and hold to strike a joint.

This is the whole trade in one motion. Rake the old mortar out, pack the new mortar in, then tool it concave so it sheds water instead of holding it.

That is the difference between a wall that lasts eighty years and a wall that lasts eight.

Straight answers

What project managers ask us first.

What happens if you fall behind?

You hear it from us before you see it on the schedule. We plan manpower against your dates before we sign, and if something moves we bring the crew size to it. The delay that hurts a GC is the one nobody mentioned until the wall was supposed to be done.

Can you match mortar on an old building?

Yes, and it is the part of restoration we care most about. We match color, sand and strength to the original and tool the joint to the profile that is already there. A repair that reads as a patch is a repair we did wrong.

Do you work through a Wisconsin winter?

Yes, with cold weather protection: heated enclosures, blankets and mortar admixtures appropriate to the mix. There are days the wall should not go up, and we will tell you which ones they are.

Will you send safety and insurance paperwork for prequalification?

Same day. EMR, OSHA log history, certificates of insurance and references. Most GCs will not let a mason bid without them, so we keep the packet current.

How early do you want to be involved?

Before the bid if you will have us. We offer masonry design assistance and layout review, and the detail that does not work is a lot cheaper to find on paper than at the wall.

How big a project can you take?

From a small commercial repair to a full corporate exterior. Call with the scope and the dates and you will get a straight answer about whether we are the right crew for it.
