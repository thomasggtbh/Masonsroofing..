Before you come

The things people ask.

Will it be crowded?

No. Every slot is capped at eighteen people across three pools. When a slot fills, it closes. You'll never wait for a spot in the water.

Are tattoos OK?

Yes, all tattoos are welcome. No covers, no stickers.

Do I need a swimsuit?

Yes, swimsuits are on in every pool. If you forget yours, we sell simple ones at the desk.

What should I bring?

Just a swimsuit and a hair tie if you need one. Towels, robes, lockers, shampoo and body wash are all here.

Can I bring kids?

In the morning slots, yes. Kids under 12 soak free with an adult. Afternoon and night slots are 16 and over.

What if I need to cancel?

Cancel up to 24 hours before your slot and you get a full refund. Inside 24 hours, you can move to another day for free.
